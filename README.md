# BAE305-SP26-Lab3
Basic Circuits

Tom Beresnyak and Brandon Rutland


Introduction
  The purpose of this lab was to develop basic circuit construction and measurement skills by building and testing DC resistor networks. A soldered series circuit and a breadboard parallel/series circuit were constructed and measured using a digital multimeter and DC power supply. Voltage and current measurements were collected to experimentally verify Ohm’s Law, Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL), and Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL).

  Additional measurements were performed to determine Thevenin and Norton equivalent values and compare calculated results with measured data. The lab emphasized proper meter usage, resistor verification, and safe soldering techniques while reinforcing fundamental circuit analysis principles.
